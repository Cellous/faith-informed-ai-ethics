Research and educational initiative exploring faith-informed moral leadership in artificial intelligence ethics, governance, and societal impact. Developed to support ethical AI literacy, community engagement, and institutional dialogue.

# Faith-Informed AI Ethics and Moral Leadership

## Overview
This repository documents research, reflections, and educational resources
exploring ethical and moral leadership perspectives on artificial intelligence.

## Purpose
To support responsible AI literacy for faith-based leaders and educators by
translating complex AI ethics issues into accessible, human-centered guidance.

## Scope
- AI ethics & governance
- Human dignity and stewardship
- Risk, accountability, and oversight
- Public understanding of AI impacts

## Methods
- Literature review
- Policy and ethics analysis
- Educational framework development
- Human-centered design principles

## Note on AI Use
AI tools were used for limited support such as copy editing and research
organization. All core ideas, structure, and conclusions are human-authored.

## Status
Conceptual research and educational initiative (non-technical).
